Core Capabilities

What Dermaire Does

Computer Vision Check-ins

Calibrated face guide ensures angle and lighting match previous photos, allowing Azure AI Vision 4.0 to detect objective changes in Erythema (redness) and surface roughness texture.

Clinical Conflict Engine

Screen cosmetic products automatically. If you introduce a strong active ingredient (e.g., Retinol) while using an exfoliator (e.g., Glycolic AHA), Dermaire alerts you and pauses the experiment.

Responsible Medical AI

Powered by Azure OpenAI GPT-4o with real-time Azure AI Content Safety. Instantly detects medical red flags (facial swelling, anaphylaxis, severe blistering) and escalates to professional emergency care.

Clinician Access Portal

Share an ephemeral, time-bounded QR code with your dermatologist. Certified clinicians can review uncompressed skin scans, trend graphs, and document verified clinical notes.

N-of-1 Scientific Method

Isolate one cosmetic variable for 14 to 28 days. Collect meaningful longitudinal personal data to determine if a product is truly improving your skin or causing silent barrier degradation.

Strict Health Data Privacy

Photos are securely encrypted in Microsoft Azure Blob Storage with ephemeral SAS tokens. Every access event is immutably audited in an append-only clinical trail.
