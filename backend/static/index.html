We Are NeoVague. Engineering Real Solutions For Complex Needs.

We are a team of four students from Faculty of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, Helwan University, who came together around one shared frustration: skincare advice online is generic, contradictory, and impossible to verify for your own skin.

Our Featured Project Meet The Team
The NeoVague Vision & Philosophy
"Good skincare decisions should come from personal data, not guesswork."

Our backgrounds span software engineering, cloud architecture, UI/UX design, and product research, which lets us cover Dermaire end-to-end — from the guided on-device camera capture and Azure-based backend, to the experiment logic and the user-facing app design. What unites us is a shared belief that evidence and personal data should empower human wellness, and we built Dermaire to prove that idea can actually work as a real, scientifically grounded product rather than just another photo-filter skin app.
