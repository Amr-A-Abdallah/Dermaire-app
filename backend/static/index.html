We Are NeoVague. Engineering Real Solutions For Complex Needs.

We are a team of four students from Faculty of Computer Science and Information Systems, Ain Shams University, who came together around one shared frustration: skincare advice online is generic, contradictory, and impossible to verify for your own skin.

Our Featured Project Meet The Team
The NeoVague Engineering Philosophy
"Good skincare decisions should come from personal data, not guesswork."

Our backgrounds span software engineering, cloud architecture, UI/UX design, and product research, which lets us cover Dermaire end-to-end — from the guided on-device camera capture and Azure-based backend, to the experiment logic and the user-facing app design. What unites us is a shared belief that good skincare decisions should come from personal data, not guesswork, and we built Dermaire to prove that idea can actually work as a real, scientifically grounded product rather than just another photo-filter skin app.

Dermaire

A clinical personal skin lab mobile app that replaces internet guesswork with an N-of-1 trial model. Tracks Erythema redness and surface roughness through normalized daily photography and Azure AI Vision 4.0, while automatically blocking dangerous active-ingredient combinations.
